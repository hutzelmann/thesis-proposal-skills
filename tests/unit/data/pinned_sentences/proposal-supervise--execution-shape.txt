## Execution shape

Single context, one pass, at most three agents: you normalize, check, judge the six substance tests, the dimensions and the workload together, decide the tier, and curate and verify the feedback, because the tier needs the tests judged side by side against the evidence bar and the points are chosen across all findings. Helpers are not part of this skill, with one exception: one fresh verifier of the finished feedback, worth its cost because it did not write what it checks. Following a sibling skill's instructions in this context is not a helper. Where the host can start helper agents, you — the full review and the only writer — always add that verifier, and only a host that insists on a workflow adds at most one adversarial check of your fail verdicts; never one agent per test, dimension, research question or claim. Where it cannot, ask the verifier's questions yourself, each separately against its fetched source, with your drafting reasoning set aside. The adversarial check informs the evidence bar; the tier stays with you and, when borderline, the professor.

A helper writes no file and never reads the submission, so no student identity reaches it; the review carries every finding, and the strengths are yours to find. The adversarial check works from `<slug>.md` with the workspace `guidelines.md` override where one exists (beside the proposal, else in the workspace root) and only the guideline sections its task needs, and returns, per substance test it examined, decisive fail, uncertain or pass — one quotable finding per failed test and, for a decisive fail, why no single revision round repairs it — then at most five findings, each with severity, location, a one-sentence problem, a one-sentence suggestion and a quote of at most one sentence, location-only duplicates merged. The verifier works from the finished feedback, `<slug>.md`, the work plan normalization moved into the notes file, and your claim ledger with its fetched evidence, with network only as far as the professor's approval reaches, and checks six things: fidelity to the proposal, claims about papers, claims about standards and regulations, contradictions inside the feedback, review vocabulary reaching the student, and asks that break the workload budget. Per disputed claim it returns the location, the claim, a verdict — wrong, overstated, imprecise or unverifiable — the evidence and a correction. No helper returns reasoning prose, strengths, restated guidelines or a rewritten feedback unless the professor asks. You decide on every verifier finding and check each sentence you change against its source.
